EDP 2 to 6 Weeks

Entrepreneurship Development Programme (EDP)

Comprehensive structured training for individuals serious about launching a enterprise. Covers business preparation, financial inputs, and formal bank linkage.

  • Idea selection & project profile creation
  • Market survey methodology & customer testing
  • PMEGP / MYSY government scheme orientation

EAP 1 to 3 Days

Entrepreneurship Awareness Programme (EAP)

Short awareness orientation workshops held across colleges and district centers to spark entrepreneurial thinking and introduce local opportunities.

  • Introduction to self-employment vs job seeking
  • Overview of district-level support systems
  • Q&A with successful local entrepreneurs

SKILL INPUTS 1 to 2 Weeks

Skill & Business Inputs Training

Specialized practical training focusing on technical, operational, and domain-specific skills required for running specific micro-enterprises.

  • Food processing & packaging techniques
  • Statutory compliance & GST registration
  • Digital marketing & accounting basics
