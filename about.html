Fostering entrepreneurship across Karnataka.

CEDOK — Center for Entrepreneurship Development of Karnataka — is an autonomous body under the Government of Karnataka, established to build entrepreneurial capacity, conduct EDP training, and empower youth to start micro-enterprises.

Awareness is only the beginning.

Entrepreneurship development involves systematically guiding an individual from an initial concept into a structured, bankable project equipped with technical, financial, and managerial capability.

CEDOK acts as the primary training and capacity-building agency in Karnataka, organizing EDPs, awareness workshops, and specialized skill inputs across all 31 districts in partnership with District Industries Centres (DICs).
