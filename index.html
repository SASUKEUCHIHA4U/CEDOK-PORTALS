Before you invest, understand the opportunity.

Successful business preparation involves rigorous evaluation beyond a initial concept. Evaluate customers, costs, and execution realities.

STEP 01

Identify

Uncover specific customer problems, market gaps, or local resource advantages.

STEP 02

Research

Assess demand signals, customer willingness to pay, pricing, and competitors.

STEP 03

Plan

Formulate project requirements, equipment, raw materials, costs, and bank scheme eligibility.

STEP 04

Connect

Engage in CEDOK EDP training and connect with your district industry office.
